## Hello

I'm Rafli, a **Cloud Engineer at Quantum Teknologi (Nusantara)** in Bandung, and a **FOSS** person by habit. My weekdays run on YAML, terminals, and whatever the cloud decided to break that morning; my weekends run on trails, camera in hand, legs complaining. This page is the space between the two.

## What I actually do

By day, I make infrastructure boring, in the best sense. I've moved workloads across AWS, LayerStack, Kubernetes, and Huawei Cloud, and I get a small, unreasonable thrill when a pipeline I tuned drops from twenty minutes to twelve. I've cut deployment lead times by 40% chasing that feeling, and it never stops being satisfying.

When something breaks at 2 a.m., I'd rather be reading the dashboard than hearing about it secondhand, so I lean on Signoz, Prometheus, and Grafana. I've also strung together CI/CD pipelines with Rancher Fleet, Jenkins, and SonarQube, the kind where a deploy stops being an event and starts being a shrug.

Before the cloud, I spent a stretch in the 5G world: OpenAirInterface, VNFs, and the half-finished frontier of open core networks. That's where I learned to enjoy systems still figuring themselves out.

## On film: hiking & trekking

Somewhere between the trail running and the writing, I started filming. Not because I'm good at it, but because a hike remembered only in my head fades too quickly. So I carry a camera uphill, point it at whatever the mountain offers, and cut it into something I can keep. A few of them live on [my YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@raflihadiaan).
